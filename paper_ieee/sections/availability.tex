\section*{Data and Code Availability}
Code, specifications, run records and result tables are at
\url{https://github.com/amanchaurasia512/Self_optimized_Agent_Ai_chunking}, tag
\texttt{paper-v1.4}. It includes the chunkers, the scorer, the analysis
scripts, the tuning and held-out set~1 score files, and
checksum files for the paper's inputs. The score files and result tables of
held-out set~2, which hold identifiers, scores and statistics only, are included
at this tag. The project's own code is under the MIT licence; that licence does not
cover material derived from the datasets, which \texttt{NOTICE.md} lists with its
source and terms. CUAD and QASPER are under CC~BY~4.0, as are the questions of
OHR-Bench and the annotations of ViDoRe~V3. UniDoc-Bench and the annotations of
FinanceBench are under CC~BY-NC~4.0 (non-commercial). MP-DocVQA is under MIT at
its source, though the copy we read states no licence. The repository of the
Indian benchmark is under Apache-2.0. The licences of the OHR-Bench, Indian
and MP-DocVQA documents themselves are unknown to us or not recorded.
ViDoRe~V3 documents keep the licences of their publishers.

Not redistributed are the PDFs, the extracted text, chunks and embeddings, and
the model answers (\texttt{answers.jsonl}). The PDFs can be
obtained from the dataset sources.
