\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{Scope.} All scored documents and questions are in English; the Hindi track
was not evaluated. The main results use one embedder, bge-m3. The second,
nomic-embed-text-v1.5, was run on tuning data only, as were the extension
chunkers. RQ1 was analysed at 1,250 tokens only; the planned analyses at 750 and
2,000 were not run. Only two parsers are compared.

\textbf{Few questions per document.} The oracle chooses from half of a
document's questions, so its choice is weak. On held-out set~2 the median
document has \RQoneHTwoMedianQuestions{} questions;
\RQoneHTwoTwoQuestionDocuments{} of \RQoneHTwoDropRuleDocuments{} documents have
two and \RQoneHTwoOneQuestionDocuments{} have one, where the gap is zero by
construction. The best single setup is chosen on the data it is scored on, which
also lowers the gap. The gap is therefore no upper bound on what a better
chooser could gain. Held-out set~1 has only \RQoneDocuments{} scorable documents.

\textbf{Questions and gold.} Many questions were generated by language models. Of
\GoldenOverallLabelled{} checked Indian tuning questions, \GoldenOverallInvalid{}
were judged invalid by one rater, with short context, and the check stopped
after its first batch; the Indian held-out questions were not checked. Comparisons use questions that every setup can
place, which hides differences in coverage between parsers. The two held-out
sets differ in gold source and gold rule, and the rule of the second was changed
after gold counts, though before any score.

\textbf{Reuse and post-hoc parts.} Held-out set~1 was used for RQ1, then for
RQ5(b), answer quality, gold-drop and size reports, and analyses by retriever
and without the Indian documents. RQ2 on tuning data was defined after the
tuning scores, RQ5(b) after the held-out scores, and the primary answer measures
(token F1, containment) after the answers existed. In M1's design, the tree
structure of most documents of the first part of held-out set~1 had been seen.

\textbf{Weaker evidence.} The RQ2 effect is not established at 2,000 tokens on
held-out set~2, and is positive there for two of three corpora.
Answer quality covers \RQfourSetups{} digital setups and one answering model,
and \AQUnrecordedAnswers{} of \AQAnswers{} answers have no run record. The scanned
results rest on \RQfiveAScannedQuestions{} shared questions for recall and
\AQScannedQuestions{} for answers. The HybridChunker result includes our span
mapping, which could not locate \ExtHybridUnlocated{} of \ExtHybridChunks{} chunks.
